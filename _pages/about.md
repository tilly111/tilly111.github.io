I am a PhD candidate in computer science in the [Cyber-Physical Systems Group](https://www.cps.uni-konstanz.de/) at the University of Konstanz, advised by Prof. [Heiko Hamann](https://heikohamann.de).

My research is on **automated machine learning for physiological time series** in cyber-physical systems.
I develop classifiers that turn living plants into environmental sensors and run them on resource-constrained sensor nodes.
I also estimate mental states from wearable physiological signals.
My dissertation automates the design of such models.
Its title is _Automated Machine Learning for Physiological Time Series Data in Cyber-physical Systems_.

During a research stay at the [National Institute of Informatics](https://www.nii.ac.jp/en/) in Tokyo, I worked on the **reliability of predictions under distribution shift**.
I contributed to a selective-classification layer that abstains from unreliable forecasts of time-series foundation models.
I studied its behavior on synthetically generated data.

A further line of my work concerns **collective behavior in multi-robot systems**.
I study how decentralized agents synchronize and monitor dynamic environments without central control.

Before Konstanz, I studied Robotics and Autonomous Systems at the University of Lübeck (B.Sc., M.Sc.) and wrote my master's thesis at IRIDIA, Université Libre de Bruxelles.
