I wrote my master's thesis at IRIDIA, Université Libre de Bruxelles, Belgium (2021–2022).
It studies collective adaptation and symmetry breaking in a swarm of Kilobots that monitors a dynamic environment.
Limited communication and slow sensing can benefit the swarm {% cite Aust2022 %}.

- I ran the physics-based simulation campaign in ARGoS on a high-performance computing cluster with SLURM.
- A run with 50 physical Kilobots on the Kilogrid demonstrated transferability. The [video](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.6611777) is on Zenodo.

Earlier, as a student research assistant in Lübeck, I contributed to simulation-based research on collaboration and interference in robot swarms {% cite Hamann2020 %}.
