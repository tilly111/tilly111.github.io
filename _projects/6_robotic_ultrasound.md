My bachelor's thesis at the University of Lübeck addresses semi-automated 2D ultrasound scanning of peripheral arteries.

- I implemented the image-based detection and tracking of the artery.
- I implemented the visual-servoing control of a KUKA LBR iiwa robot arm.
