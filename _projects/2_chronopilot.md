In the EU Horizon 2020 FET project ChronoPilot (2022–2025), I worked toward an adaptive human-machine feedback system.

- I estimated mental states and subjective time perception from time-synchronized wearable signals. These include electrocardiography and heart rate, electrodermal activity, and eye tracking.
- I helped design and run the user studies. I tested whether physiological features differ between task conditions.
- I validated the models across subjects on unseen participants.
- I assisted in further human-robot interaction studies.
