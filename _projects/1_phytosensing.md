From 2022 to 2024, I worked in the EU Horizon 2020 FET project WatchPlant.
There, living plants serve as biohybrid sensors for environmental monitoring.
Their electrophysiological signals change when the environment stresses them.
My work detects these stress responses with machine learning, directly on the sensor node.

- I applied automated machine learning and developed deep-learning classifiers for plant electrophysiological signals. They detect heat and ozone exposure with a sensitivity of up to 0.98.
- I deployed the classifiers on low-power embedded sensor nodes with ExecuTorch. I co-developed the on-device C/C++ code and rebuilt an operation missing from the runtime out of existing primitives.
- I operated the sensor nodes in long-term outdoor field deployments and analyzed the resulting continuous, noisy measurement streams.
