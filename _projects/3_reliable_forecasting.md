From April to July 2026, I was a visiting researcher at the [National Institute of Informatics](https://www.nii.ac.jp/en/) in Tokyo, Japan.

- I evaluated pretrained time-series foundation models in zero-shot inference on financial time series.
- I developed reliability estimation for their forecasts under temporal distribution shift.
- I designed a selective-classification layer that abstains from unreliable forecasts. It uses data-efficient tabular classifiers. I assessed it with calibration analysis and risk-coverage curves.
- I benchmarked the approach on regime-aware synthetic data under controlled distribution shifts. A modular Python pipeline runs thousands of parameterized evaluation jobs.
