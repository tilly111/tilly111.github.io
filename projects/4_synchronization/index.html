Decentralized Synchronization

Collective timing without a central clock.

Fireflies flash in unison without a conductor. I study how agents with firefly-inspired coupling reach collective timing without a central clock. Our results show that noise and sparse connectivity can improve synchronization rather than degrade it (Aust et al., 2026).
